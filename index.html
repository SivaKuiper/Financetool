💡 Two Easy Ways to Import:
Option 1: Upload Excel file directly (.xlsx, .xls, .csv)
Option 2: Copy & paste from Excel (10 columns: Invoice No | Customer | Country | Material | Inv Amount ($) | Inv Amount (₹) | Received ($) | Pending ($) | ETA Date | Due Date)

Option 1: Upload Excel File

Option 2: Paste from Excel

Required columns (in order):
Invoice Number | Customer Name | Material | Invoice Amount USD | Invoice Amount INR | Received USD | Pending Amount | ETA Date | Due Date

📝 How to Copy from Excel:

  1. Open your Excel file
  2. Select ALL data including the header row
  3. Press Ctrl+C (Windows) or Cmd+C (Mac)
  4. Click in the text area above
  5. Press Ctrl+V (Windows) or Cmd+V (Mac)
  6. Click "Import Pasted Data"

💡 Tip: The columns will align automatically when you paste - don't worry about spacing!
